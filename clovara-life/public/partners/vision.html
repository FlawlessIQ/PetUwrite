Why we exist

We build for the pet.

Great companies are customer-centric. Ours is pet-centric — because our customer can't read the label, compare the plans, or tell you where it hurts.

So every choice at Clovara starts with one question: does this make his life easier, longer, or better? If the answer is no, we don't build it. If a product doesn't earn its place in his routine, we don't sell it. If the science is only half-sure, we say so — out loud, on the screen.

His family gets the second promise: you'll always know where he is, what he needs, and what's coming next. No guessing at midnight. No panic at the vet's front desk. No fine print when it matters most.

Everything we make speaks for him."

Care that fits today

Made for the dog he is right now.

A dog doesn't need forty thousand products. He needs the few right things for this exact chapter of his life — and a family that isn't left guessing which ones.

When Max was a puppy, his shelf held teething chews and a crate sized to grow with him. Today it holds hip & joint support — because he's six, because he's a golden, and because his vet flagged his hips two years ago. Clovara's shelf is built from his plan, not from a warehouse. Every product says why it was chosen for him — and when he outgrows something, it quietly leaves the shelf.
Members pay less on everything — and earn rewards on the products that keep him healthy.

The daily rhythm

He'll tell us — before he can tell you.

Most of a dog's healthy years are won in the quiet part: weight held steady, teeth brushed, walks kept up. Small things, done consistently, for years. Clovara makes the small things effortless — a score his family can see, streaks that reward the habits that matter, one glance each morning.

And it watches for the things a family can't. This week Max's activity is down 18% — years of his own rhythm say that's unusual. His family hears about it while it's still a question, not an emergency. And every birthday, his plan is re-drawn from the year he actually lived: "Max gained half a healthy year." Families frame that.
Someone who never forgets

Ask at 11pm. It remembers everything.

"Max has been limping after walks." Every pet parent knows this moment — the worry, the search results, the guessing.

Clovara answers differently, because it knows him: it recalls the hip note from his 2024 checkup, connects it to what's happening now, tells his family what to watch for — and books a video vet with a one-page summary of his history already prepared. It informs and connects; it never diagnoses. Licensed vets decide his care.
Private by design. These conversations exist to help Max — they're never used in underwriting or claims.
When something goes wrong

Protected from day one — when it means the most.

Puppies eat socks. Seniors slow down. And the best time to protect a dog is before he has a history — covered young, nothing that comes later is a "pre-existing condition."

Because Clovara has known Max since his first week, protecting him took one tap, not one afternoon — his plan already held the answers. And when the sock happened, his family sent a photo of the vet bill and had their answer in hours, not weeks.
Coverage that completes the plan — not a separate thing to shop for, compare, and dread.
Routine care, given back. His wellness rider returns money for the visits and cleanings he should be having anyway.
Prevention and protection, finally together. The same plan that keeps him well is the one that pays when he isn't.
